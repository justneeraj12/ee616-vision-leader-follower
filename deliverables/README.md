## Earlier and supporting material

The files containing `Initial`, `Toy`, `Baseline`, `Industry_Application`, or
`Project_Update` document earlier stages or supporting explanations. They are
retained to preserve development history. Their preliminary results must not be
mixed with the later ROS 2 and Gazebo evidence without identifying the source.

## Evidence rule

A video or visual demonstration is not the primary numerical evidence. Accepted
results are indexed in `../docs/FINAL_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md` and stored in the
machine-readable `../work/ros2_ws/results/` directories.
