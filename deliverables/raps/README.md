# RAPS Fall 2026 Poster Draft

This directory contains the editable RAPS poster draft and two rendered proofs:

- `RAPS_Fall_2026_Poster_Draft_v2.pptx`: editable 36 by 24 inch PowerPoint;
- `RAPS_Fall_2026_Poster_Draft_v2.pdf`: print-layout proof; and
- `RAPS_Fall_2026_Poster_Draft_v2.png`: quick visual preview.

The draft uses the accepted November 9 post-freeze evidence. It distinguishes
10 of 10 route completions from the stricter result that only four of nine
mapped runs passed every check. Five chain runs missed only the unchanged 90%
`TRACK`-fraction check.

The airport text is labeled as a future application hypothesis. The poster does
not claim airport trials, throughput or cost savings, physical robots, person
safety, industrial certification, production readiness, or autonomous cargo
handling.

The supplied Google Slides template was view-only. The local PowerPoint draft
recreates its single-page size, section layout, typography hierarchy, and green
accent with editable elements. Neeraj Kumar Kanchani should review every line,
figure, and number before printing or submission.

Run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` from this directory to verify the three artifacts.
